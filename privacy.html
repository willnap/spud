Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

Spud is a private personal assistant run by Will Napier for himself and a small number of invited people. This policy covers the data Spud reads when you connect a Google account.

What Spud accesses

  • Gmail (https://mail.google.com/): to show your email in Spud, and to send email when you ask it to.
  • Google Contacts, read-only (contacts.readonly and contacts.other.readonly): to recognise the people you talk to, and to show their photos.
  • Google Calendar, read-only (calendar.readonly), where it is granted: to show your plans alongside your messages.
  • Your Google account email address (openid, email): to know which account is connected.

How it is used

Your data is used only to provide Spud's features to you. It is stored encrypted on Spud's private server and on your own devices. It is processed by Anthropic's Claude models to understand and organise your messages for you. It is never sold, never used for advertising, never shared with anyone else, and never used by Spud to train AI models.

Spud's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can disconnect Spud at any time from your Google account's third-party access page. To have your data deleted, email the address below; it is deleted from Spud's server and backups.
